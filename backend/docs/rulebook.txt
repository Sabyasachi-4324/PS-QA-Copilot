Treasure Road — QA Test Scenarios
Feature Overview (User Perspective)

A user joins the Treasure Road event, earns dice (ammo), rolls to move along a 99-tile board split into 3 laps,
collects tile rewards along the way, claims milestone rewards at the end of each lap, and cashes out a Vault
reward at the end. A Super Pass can be bought for bonus lap rewards.
